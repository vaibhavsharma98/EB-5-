End-to-End Investor Services

A defined advisory scope for evaluating EB-5, coordinating independent professionals, and planning a potential U.S. transition.

Structured advisory support

End-to-End EB-5 Consultancy

The Calculus provides a defined scope of EB-5 advisory support — from preliminary readiness and document organisation through professional coordination and settlement planning.

Preliminary Readiness Review

A non-legal review of goals, investment readiness, family needs, and likely documentation questions before attorney advice.

Project Comparison Support

Organise offering information so you and your independent legal, securities, tax, and financial advisers can compare structure, risks, fees, conflicts, job models, and exit assumptions.

Documentation Assistance

Help organise the documents requested by your immigration attorney. The attorney remains responsible for legal strategy and the evidence filed with USCIS.

Source of Funds Guidance

Coordinate document collection with the investor’s Indian tax, FEMA/foreign-exchange, banking, and U.S. immigration advisers.

Attorney Coordination

Keep communication and document requests organised between the family and its independently engaged U.S. immigration attorney. The attorney alone provides U.S. immigration legal advice.

Banking Assistance

Practical introductions and account setup guidance during your transition to the United States.

Foreign Remittance Support

Administrative coordination with the authorised-dealer bank and qualified Indian FEMA/tax advisers. We do not provide a legal or tax opinion on remittance eligibility.

USCIS Process Updates

Regular status updates and timeline tracking throughout your case. We keep you informed about reported milestones — from petition receipt through the applicable adjudication and visa process.

Relocation Planning

City selection, housing search, schooling research, neighbourhood orientation, and comprehensive soft-landing support for your family's transition to the United States.

Continued Family Support

Our relationship does not end when your Green Card arrives. We continue to support your family with ongoing guidance, professional referrals, and advisory for years to come.

Our professional consultancy supports every stage of your EB-5 journey.
