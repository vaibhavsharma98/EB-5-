Information we collect

When you request a consultation, complete the readiness questionnaire, unlock the Journey page, call, email, or contact us through WhatsApp, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, country code, message, readiness answers, and any information you choose to provide.

Our hosting and security providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, requested pages, and diagnostic logs. This site does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioural advertising trackers.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange consultations, understand the assistance requested, protect the site from abuse, maintain records of communications, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Submitting a form does not create an adviser-client, attorney-client, fiduciary, or investment relationship.

When information may be shared

Information may be processed by service providers that support website hosting, email delivery, communications, and security. We do not sell personal information. We will not share your case details with an independent attorney, accountant, project representative, or other professional for follow-up without your direction or another lawful basis.

WhatsApp, Google Maps, and other external links are operated by third parties under their own privacy terms. Choosing those services may transmit information outside India.

Retention and security

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet or email transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for adults evaluating investment immigration and is not directed to children under 18. Please do not submit a child’s personal information through the website.
