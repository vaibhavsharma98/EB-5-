## Claims removed or rewritten

- Removed the October 2026 countdown and “window closing” urgency.
- Removed India ranking, annual-growth, “500+ families,” and similar unsupported
  market or client-volume claims.
- Removed unnamed “tier-one”, audited partner, approval-record, and project
  quality claims.
- Removed ISO, ICEF, AFECI, “first”, “largest”, “only”, and pioneering claims
  pending evidence and current-status verification.
- Removed placeholder testimonials and all implied client outcomes.
- Replaced universal timelines, faster-processing promises, automatic
  citizenship language, guaranteed tuition/education benefits, and broad H-1B
  concurrent-filing language with conditional statements.
- Reframed legal, tax, securities, remittance, mortgage, insurance, and
  real-estate work as independent professional review, referral, or coordination.
- Reframed “eligibility check” as a preliminary, non-legal readiness review.
- Tied post-arrival and continuing support to the signed engagement scope.

## Company statements not independently verified

The website still uses company-supplied statements about a business history
dating to 1985, 41 years of continuity in 2026, the Connaught Circus office,
leadership identity/biography, phone number, and contact email. Before launch,
the owner should retain dated evidence and written publication approval for each
statement. These statements have not been presented as 41 years of EB-5
experience.

No credential, client count, success rate, testimonial, partner performance,
professional licence, ranking, or exclusive arrangement should be added without
a dated substantiation file, conflict/compensation review, and permission from
the named party.

## Update rule

The dated program-status section on `disclosures.html` must be reviewed every
month against the newest Visa Bulletin. Re-review the full site after any change
to USCIS rules, legislation, RBI guidance, service scope, referral relationships,
fees, conflicts, privacy practices, or company identity.
